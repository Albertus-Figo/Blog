ARTICLE 3

Growing as a Coder

Practice and learning from mistakes can help you improve

When I first started coding, I mostly focused on getting my programs to work. As I gained more experience, I started to pay more attention to how I write my code and how I approach a problem. I began to realize that coding is not only about knowing the right commands, but also about finding different ways to solve a problem and using the most suitable and efficient method.

One thing that has helped me improve my coding skills is practicing regularly and trying to solve problems on my own before looking for the answer. Sometimes I still get stuck, but instead of giving up, I take my time and try to find different approaches. I also started noticing patterns in problems that I had seen before, which made it easier to understand and solve new ones.

Looking back, I think growing as a coder is mostly about experience. The more problems I solve, the more I understand how to approach them, and I become more comfortable with trying things I have not done before. I still have a lot to learn, but I have learned that improving does not happen all at once. It comes from practice that takes time, learning from mistakes, and not giving up.
